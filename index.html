About Perfumy & The Project

Redefining Modern Fragrance With Artisanal Craftsmanship

PERFUMY is a bespoke online fragrance boutique and digital showcase engineered to redefine how luxury scents are discovered and experienced. Designed in London, our platform bridges cutting-edge modern e-commerce web technology with the centuries-old artistry of haute parfumerie.

Every signature bottle is formulated by master perfumers utilizing rare cold-pressed floral absolutes, sustainably harvested resins, and aged wood extracts. Compounded at Extrait de Parfum concentrations, our perfumes ensure a rich, unfolding olfactory experience with unmatched longevity and zero toxic additives.
